Juha Kerminen

UX/UI & Product Designer

I make complex services feel obvious. I design digital services and web applications with dense workflows, business rules and edge cases, and turn them into interfaces people can use and developers can build.

Approach

I work where design meets the system underneath it. Most of my projects start with rules, data, legacy constraints and several kinds of users sharing one process. My job is to understand that system well enough that the people using it don’t have to.

I design in the real material: real content, real states and real edge cases, tested in prototypes rather than presentations. I work closely with developers, using components, tokens and specs that hold up in code. I use AI tools to explore and prototype faster, and keep the judgment calls my own.
