Senior UI/UX Designer

I make complex services feel obvious. Senior UI/UX designer with over 15 years in digital design, from web and brand work to service design and data-heavy web applications.

Approach

My background spans UI/UX, service design and graphic design, so I can take an idea from first concept to a finished, consistent experience across every touchpoint. Most of my work sits where user needs, business goals and technical limits meet, and my job is to find the version that works for all three.

I also have years of hands-on frontend development behind me. That makes me easy for developers to work with: I design things that can be built, speak their language, and care about the details that hold up in code. Lately I’ve been exploring AI-assisted design workflows to prototype and iterate faster.
