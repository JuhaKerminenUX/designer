03About

About

I spend most of my time understanding how a service really works, so the people using it don’t have to.

Approach

  1. Understand the system first

    Rules, data, constraints and the people inside the process — before pixels.

  2. Design in the real material

    Real content, real states, real edge cases. Prototypes over presentations.

  3. Ship with the team

    Shared language with developers: components, tokens, and specs that hold up in code.
